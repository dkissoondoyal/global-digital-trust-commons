# Initial governance

**Status: founder-led experimental project.** GDTC is an independently developed, open-source public-benefit initiative originating in Mauritius. No international partner, ICANN structure, Mozilla Foundation or other organisation is designated as project co-owner, sponsor or governing member by virtue of an invitation or existing relationship.

## Decision process (provisional)

- The initial maintainer reviews public issues and pull requests for correctness, user safety, accessibility and project scope.
- Any security-sensitive change requires documented review before release; external independent review is an objective, not a completed milestone.
- Architectural and scope changes are proposed publicly for comment. Private pilot data and credentials remain confidential.
- Contributors retain credit for their work under the project licence.
- A participant-governed charter, maintainer admission process, conflict-of-interest policy and succession plan are **planned**, not yet established.

The goal is to transition from a founder-led demonstration to community stewardship as people and organisations voluntarily join.
