# Community code of conduct

GDTC welcomes constructive participation regardless of location, background, language or technical experience. Be respectful, patient, accessible and evidence-led. Harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing and unsolicited disclosure of confidential security information are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may moderate or remove inappropriate issues, comments or contributions, and may restrict participation to protect contributors and volunteers. Report concerns privately to repository maintainers when possible, especially when a report includes personal or security-sensitive information. This is an initial community policy, subject to review with actual contributors; it is not a claim that a formal governing board exists.
